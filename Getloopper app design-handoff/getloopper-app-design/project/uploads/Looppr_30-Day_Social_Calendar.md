# Looppr — 30-Day Launch Content Calendar
**@getlooppr · getlooppr.com · Launch: Tuesday, July 22, 2026 · OKC Metro**

Covers **July 14 – August 12, 2026** in four phases:

| Phase | Dates | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Countdown | Jul 14–21 | Build curiosity + waitlist before launch |
| 2 — Launch Week | Jul 22–28 | Convert attention into first orders |
| 3 — Proof | Jul 29–Aug 4 | Social proof, reorders, referrals |
| 4 — Momentum | Aug 5–12 | Habit content, B2B, month-one story |

- Instagram + TikTok are the primary platforms. Facebook gets community-tone versions (no hashtags). X gets the short version. YouTube Shorts = repost the best TikTok of the week.
- Every video: hook in the first 2 seconds, captions on, shot on phone, unpolished founder energy.
- Every caption ends with: **getlooppr.com** (link in bio).
- Best posting windows for OKC: 7–9 AM and 6–8 PM CT.
